Dictionary: stroke.dict.yaml, preserved verbatim; AUTHORS and upstream README.md included.
The five strokes are h=horizontal, s=vertical, p=left-falling, n=dot/right-falling, z=turn.
Lens performs local prefix/wildcard lookup and ranks common characters using the separately
licensed pinyin_simp dictionary. It does not embed the Rime engine or modify this data.
The full editable data is included in the APK assets and source tree.
